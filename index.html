Reflection

What did you experience?

There are no correct answers. Please respond based on whatever came to mind while viewing the environments.

1. What, if anything, did you think you were looking at during the experience?
2. Did any of the environments feel familiar to you?
3. If something felt familiar, what do you think contributed to that feeling?
4. Did you associate any environment with a place, object, event, memory, or feeling? If so, which one?
5. Which visual element affected your interpretation the most?
6. Which environment gave you the most room to form your own interpretation?
7. Was any environment too obvious, too vague, or difficult to interpret?
8. How would you describe the overall experience in a few words?
Anything else you would like to tell me?

Responses are collected for a Visual Literacy class project. Please do not include your name or other identifying information.

Memory in Light

Thank you.

This project explores how visual ambiguity, color, light, and atmosphere may create conditions for personal association and autobiographical memory.

The environments were intentionally left unresolved so that viewers could form their own interpretations rather than being told what they were supposed to see or feel.
