Visual environment study

Before you begin

You’ll move through four visual environments. Spend as much or as little time with each as you like.

After each environment, you’ll answer two short questions about what you noticed. Use the arrows to move forward or return to an earlier screen.

Your responses stay saved while you move through the study. There are no correct interpretations.

Final reflection

Looking across all four

Think about the environments as a group. Your earlier responses will remain saved if you go back to look again.

Did any of the environments feel familiar to you?
If yes or unsure, which environment or environments felt familiar?
Did any environment bring a specific place, object, event, memory, or feeling to mind? If so, tell me about it.
Which visual element affected your interpretations the most overall?
Which environment gave you the most room to form your own interpretation?
Was any environment too obvious, too vague, or difficult to interpret?
How would you describe the overall experience in a few words?
Anything else you would like to tell me?

Responses are collected for a Visual Literacy class project. Please do not include your name or other identifying information.

Memory in Light

Thank you.

This project explores how visual ambiguity, color, light, and atmosphere may create conditions for personal association and autobiographical memory.

The environments were intentionally left unresolved so that viewers could form their own interpretations rather than being told what they were supposed to see or feel.
